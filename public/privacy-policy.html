Matchkeeper

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Matchkeeper is a community-management app for padel and football groups — organizing events, tracking skill ratings, and running round-robin match sessions. This page explains what information Matchkeeper collects, how it's used, and the choices you have.

Information we collect

When you create a Matchkeeper account (email/password, Google, or Apple sign-in), we collect:

  • Account info: name, nickname, email address, and (if you add one) phone number and gender.
  • Profile info you choose to add: area/city, an optional InstaPay payment link, and a break-time preference.
  • Activity data: the communities and events you join, match results and scores, your skill rating (USR) and its history, and team history.
  • Payment confirmations: for communities that track dues, a record that a payment was made (amount, method, date) — Matchkeeper does not process card payments or store card details itself.
  • Push notification token: if you enable notifications, a device token used to deliver event reminders and alerts.

Location: if you tap "How far is it?" on a venue, your device's current location is used for a single one-time distance calculation (via the free, open-source OpenStreetMap routing service) and is never stored or sent anywhere else.

We do not use advertising or third-party analytics SDKs, and we don't sell or share your data with advertisers.

How your information is used

  • To run the app itself: showing you events, standings, and your match history.
  • To send you event reminders, registration updates, and other notifications you've opted into.
  • To let community admins organize events and track who's registered, checked in, and paid.

Who can see your information

  • Your nickname, skill rating, and match history are visible to other members of communities you belong to, and to that community's admins — this is the core purpose of the app.
  • Your email address and phone number are private — shown as text only to you and the Matchkeeper platform admin. Other community/event admins can still reach you (call, SMS, or WhatsApp) through the app without ever seeing the number itself.

Where your information is stored

Matchkeeper is built on Google Firebase (Authentication, Firestore database, Cloud Functions, and Cloud Messaging for push notifications), which stores and processes data on Google Cloud infrastructure.

Account deletion

You can delete your account at any time from inside the app: Settings → Account → Delete My Account. This immediately and permanently:

  • Removes your name, email, phone number, gender, location, and payment link.
  • Closes your login — you won't be able to sign back in to that account.

Your match results and standings are kept, shown under "Deleted Player" instead of your name — other players' own statistics (head-to-head records, standings) depend on that shared history, so it isn't erased, only disconnected from your identity.

Children's privacy

Matchkeeper is intended for use by community organizers and adult players, and is not directed at children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes in a meaningful way, we'll update the date at the top of this page.
